Zero Trust Maturity

Where does this organization actually sit?

CISA's Zero Trust Maturity Model breaks Zero Trust into five pillars, each progressing through four stages. Set where the customer sits on each, live in the conversation, and see the shape of their posture take form.

Zero Trust isn't a product. It's a maturity curve — most organizations are further along in one or two pillars than the rest, and that imbalance is usually where the risk concentrates.

Framework: CISA, Zero Trust Maturity Model 2.0.

Overall posture
Traditional
Zero Trust hasn't started yet — most controls are perimeter-based and trust is implicit once inside.
Also part of the model
CISA's framework layers three cross-cutting capabilities across all five pillars above — they're what make the pillars work together rather than as five separate programs.
Visibility & Analytics
Continuous insight into user, device, and network activity — the telemetry every pillar's access decisions depend on.
Automation & Orchestration
Policy enforcement and response happen automatically, without waiting on a manual ticket or review.
Governance
The policies and risk tolerances that keep all five pillars aligned and consistently enforced.

This is a conversation starter, not a scored audit — the value is in seeing where the pillars are uneven, together.
